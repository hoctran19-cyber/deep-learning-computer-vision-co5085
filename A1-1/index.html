Dataset characteristics

Variation in food images

Food-101 covers 101 food categories with visual differences in shape, color, ingredients, and presentation. Images may vary in camera angle, lighting, background, and plating.

The original training set was intentionally not cleaned and may contain label noise and visual inconsistencies. The original test labels were manually reviewed. These characteristics make food recognition a useful classification task for comparing convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with Vision Transformers; difficulty can still vary across individual classes and images.

Connection to the experiment

CNN and Transformer protocol

Architectures: ResNet-50 and ViT-B/16.

Training regimes: from scratch, frozen backbone, partial fine-tuning, and full fine-tuning.

Evaluation: accuracy and macro-F1; training time and trainable parameters where feasible.

This is a proposed protocol, not an experimental report. No training or evaluation results are available yet. Read the full A1.1 protocol →
